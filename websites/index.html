Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is included in the free first year?+

Your domain, hosting, technical maintenance and fixes for genuine website bugs are all covered for 12 months after launch.

Can I choose monthly or yearly?+

Yes. Monthly spreads the package across 12 payments. Yearly covers the same first-year package in one payment and gives you the lower total price.

Is every website mobile-friendly?+

Yes. Every package is designed and tested for mobile and desktop, with layouts shaped for each screen rather than simply shrunk down.

Are online stores or special systems included?+

Standard business website features are included within the package scope. Shops, booking systems and advanced integrations are scoped separately before work begins.
