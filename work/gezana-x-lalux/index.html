Selected work / 03 / Event collaboration

Gezana × LaLux.

Two nightlife identities united in one electric motion piece.

Discipline
Event motion design
Format
Nightlife collaboration visual
Studio
SPNVISUALZ / Oslo

Two identities. One night signal.

The Gezana × LaLux piece brings two established names into one motion system without flattening either identity. Scale, rhythm and contrast give both marks space while the overall frame reads as one event world.

The result is direct, electric and built for quick recognition—the kind of motion that can introduce a collaboration before the full campaign even begins.
