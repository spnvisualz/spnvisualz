Selected work / 01 / Frozen identity

Icekiid.

Ice-built lettering and sharp stage motion designed to land with impact.

Discipline
Motion identity
Format
Animated artist visual
Studio
SPNVISUALZ / Oslo

A frozen mark built to move.

The Icekiid piece turns frozen lettering into a compact, high-impact motion identity. The visual language is built around a strong silhouette, sharp texture and timing that keeps the name readable while the form feels carved from ice.

It represents the artist-facing side of SPNVISUALZ: clear identity first, then motion, atmosphere and polish working together as one visual.
