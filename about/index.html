SPNVISUALZ / About

A motion-first
studio in Oslo.

SPNVISUALZ builds visual identities that are designed to move. Logos, animated marks, intros and brand visuals for artists, brands and events — made to look right the moment they appear on a screen.

Who is behind it

SPNVISUALZ is an independent creative studio based in Oslo, Norway, led by creative director CHUBAE. Work is produced directly by the studio rather than routed through a chain of subcontractors, which is what keeps the turnaround short and the visual language consistent from one project to the next.

The studio works remotely with clients regardless of location. Most projects start and finish over email, with files delivered in the formats each platform actually needs.

What the studio makes

The work sits in the space between graphic design and motion design. A logo is treated as something that will end up on a screen — in a reel, on a stage, at the front of a video — so it is designed with that arrival in mind.

  • Logo design — custom marks built around a name, sound or business, including the secondary marks and social icon needed to stay consistent.
  • Animated logos — an existing mark brought to life as a polished reveal for posts, promos, screens and video.
  • Intros — short cinematic openers for a channel, event or release.
  • Motion graphics and visuals — looping visuals and motion pieces built for social playback and live screens.
  • Brand visual identity — a coordinated set of visual assets so a brand holds together across every touchpoint.
  • Websites — premium sites for artists and small businesses, handled as a dedicated service.

How a project runs

A project starts with a short brief: what the thing is for, where it will be shown, and what it needs to feel like. From there the studio produces a first version, takes one focused round of feedback, and delivers final files.

Pricing is published openly on the main page rather than quoted privately, so it is clear before anyone commits. Delivery times are quoted per project at the point of enquiry and depend on scope and current queue.

Writing and process notes

The Visual Lab is where the studio publishes case studies and practical write-ups — how a particular identity was built, what a motion loop needs in order to hold attention, and what separates a brand that looks expensive from one that does not. It is written from the studio's own project work rather than assembled from general advice.
